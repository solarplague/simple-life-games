Overview

This policy explains what information Simple Life Games ("we", "us") collects when you play our games, currently Ninja Rooftops for Android, how we use it, and the choices you have. It also covers this website.

Short version: your progress is saved on your phone. If you sign in with Google Play Games it is also saved to your Google account. Chat and clans store the ninja name and messages you choose to share. Ads and purchases are handled by Google.

We do not ask for your real name, address, phone number, photos, contacts or precise location, and the game has no access to your payment details.

How we use it

  • To run the game: saving progress, leaderboards, achievements, chat, clans and events.
  • To show rewarded ads that you choose to watch, and to give you the reward.
  • To deliver and restore purchases.
  • To keep chat and clans safe: enforcing names and message rules and acting on reports.
  • To fix bugs and improve the game.

We do not sell your personal information.

Services we use

These Google services process data on our behalf under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Play Games Services: sign-in, cloud save, leaderboards and achievements.
  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore): ninja names, chat, clans and referrals.
  • Google AdMob: rewarded ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's Settings under Google, then Ads.
  • Google Play Billing: in-app purchases.

Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. How Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Chat, clans and safety

Messages you send in global or clan chat can be seen by other players. Please don't share personal information in chat. Names and messages are filtered, players can report messages, and we may remove content or restrict accounts that break the rules. Clan names and ninja names are public.

Children

Ninja Rooftops is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

How long we keep data

Game progress stays on your device until you reset the game or uninstall it. Cloud saves stay in your Google account until you delete them. Chat messages are stored so recent chat can be shown and reports can be handled, and you can ask us to delete yours at any time. Clan and ninja-name records are kept while your account is active.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • Reset in the game: Settings, then Reset Account, wipes your progress on the device.
  • Cloud save: you can remove Ninja Rooftops data from your Google account in the Google Play Games app.
  • Ads: reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your phone's settings.
  • Delete everything we store: email us from the contact below with your ninja name and we will delete your name, chat messages, clan and referral records within 30 days.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and stored with Google's infrastructure. Our database rules only let a player change their own records. No method of storage is perfect, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game changes. The date at the top shows the latest version. Important changes will also be announced in the game or on our Discord.
