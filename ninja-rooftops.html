Features

Simple to play. Lots to chase.

Endless rooftops

Tap to jump, tap again in the air. Land on every roof to climb, and hit the centre for PERFECT combos and bigger coin hauls.

Chests worth the hype

Skin, trail and aura chests with a full rarity reveal. Odds are always shown, and legendary drops feel legendary.

Pets that fly with you

21 pets to collect, each with its own bonus. Level them up with treats you find on the rooftops.

Clans and bosses

Team up with up to 100 players, take down five clan bosses, each with its own music, and share the rewards.

Dojo Pass seasons

A new 28-day season with 30 tiers of rewards on the free track and exclusive items on premium.

Hardcore mode

One life, no checkpoints. Reach floors 500, 1,500, 3,000 and 5,000 to earn four elemental skins you can only get here.
