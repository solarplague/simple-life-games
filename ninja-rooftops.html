Features

Simple to play. Lots to chase.

One tap, endless rooftops

Tap to jump, tap again in the air. Land on every roof to climb, and nail the centre for PERFECT combos.

Chests worth the hype

Crack open skin, trail and aura chests with a full rarity reveal. Legendary drops really feel legendary.

Pets that fly with you

21 collectible pets, each with its own bonus. Level them up with treats you find on the rooftops.

Clans and bosses

Team up with up to 100 players, take down daily and weekly clan bosses, and share the chest rewards.

Dojo Pass seasons

A new 28-day season with 30 tiers of rewards on the free track, plus exclusive items on premium.

Leaderboards and achievements

Compete on Google Play leaderboards for best run, total roofs and longest streak, and unlock 12 achievements.
