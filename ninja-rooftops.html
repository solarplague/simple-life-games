The way of the rooftop

Simple to play. Hard to master.

Endless rooftops

Tap to jump, tap again to double jump. Land dead center for PERFECT combos and bigger coin hauls, and watch the city change every ten roofs.

Chests worth opening

Skin, trail and aura chests with a full rarity reveal. Odds are always shown, and an Epic or better is guaranteed at least every 15 chests.

Robo pets

23 robo pets fly beside you, and every one gives a bonus. Hatch them from the Robo Pet Chest and level them up as you climb.

50 Guardians

Every hundred floors a Guardian blocks the way. Beat them to claim their legendary gear sets, one dimension at a time.

Duel Arena

Race a real player for 30 seconds. Most roofs wins, and you can put up to 250 honor on the line.

The Duel Store

Eight caped skins, five trails and five auras that exist nowhere else. Honor buys them, and your duel record unlocks them.

Clans and bosses

Join a clan of up to 100 ninjas, chat, take down five clan bosses together and share the rewards.

Hardcore mode

One life, no checkpoints. Reach floors 500, 1,500, 3,000 and 5,000 for four elemental skins you can only earn here.
