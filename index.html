About the studio

Made for the five minutes you have, and the hours you didn't plan on.

Simple Life Games is an independent studio. We build games that are easy to learn, look great on any phone, and treat players fairly.

Easy to pick up

Every game starts with one simple action. Mastery comes from playing, not from reading a tutorial.

Fair to play

Ads are something you choose to watch for a reward. Progress comes from playing, and nothing you earn is ever taken away.

Built with players

Our testers and Discord community shape every update, from new features to the tiniest balance tweak.

Come hang out with the community

Chat with other players, report bugs, vote on new features and get early looks at what we're building.
