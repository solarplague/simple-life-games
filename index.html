About Simple Life Games

Games that are simple to start and hard to put down.

We're an independent studio in Levittown, Pennsylvania, making mobile games for anyone with a minute to spare and a reason to come back.

  1. One tap to start

    Every game begins with a single simple action. You learn it by playing, not by reading a tutorial.

  2. Fair to everyone

    Ads are only ever something you choose to watch for a reward. Progress comes from playing, and drop odds are always shown.

  3. Made with players

    Our community shapes every update, from new features to small balance changes.
