The studio

How we make games

Simple Life Games is a small independent studio. These are the rules we build by.

One tap to start

Every game begins with a single simple action. You learn it by playing, not by reading a tutorial.

Fair to everyone

Ads are only ever something you choose to watch for a reward. Progress comes from playing, and drop odds are always shown.

Made with players

Our players and Discord community shape every update, from new features to small balance changes.

Come hang out with us

Chat with other players, report bugs, vote on what we build next and see new games before anyone else.
