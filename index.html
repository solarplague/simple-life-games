The studio

Made for the five minutes you have, and the hour you didn't plan on.

Simple Life Games is a small independent studio. We build games that are easy to learn, look great on any phone and treat players fairly.

Easy to pick up

Every game starts with one simple action. You learn by playing, not by reading a tutorial.

Fair to play

Ads are something you choose to watch for a reward. Progress comes from playing, and nothing you earn is taken away.

Built with players

Our testers and Discord community shape every update, from big features to the tiniest balance tweak.

Come hang out with us

Chat with other players, report bugs, vote on what we build next and get early looks at new games.
