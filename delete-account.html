Ninja Rooftops by Simple Life Games · How to ask us to delete your account and the data we store

Request deletion

  1. Email waterfeelscool@gmail.com with the subject Delete my Ninja Rooftops account.
  2. Include your ninja name (shown on your profile in the game), and your Google Play Games name if you signed in. This is how we find your records.
  3. We confirm by email and delete your data within 30 days. We may ask a question to make sure the request comes from the account owner.

Email a deletion request

You do not need to delete the game first, and you can ask even if you no longer have the game installed.

What we delete

  • Your ninja name and public player profile (level, title, scores, clan and outfit)
  • Chat messages you sent in global chat, clan chat and party chat, and your private messages
  • Your friends list, friend requests, party invites and duel challenges
  • Your clan membership and clan activity records (if you lead a clan, leadership passes to the next member; a clan with no other members is removed)
  • Your duel arena and dojo hall presence, and friend referral records
  • Your Firebase account ID and the link between it and your records

What we keep

  • Purchase records are kept by Google Play, not by us, as required for payments and refunds. Deleting your account does not refund purchases.
  • Message reports filed about other players may be kept for up to 90 days so we can finish reviewing them, then deleted.
  • Leaderboard scores are stored by Google Play Games. You can hide or remove them from the Google Play Games app.

Nothing else is kept after deletion.

Data on your phone and in your Google account

  • In the game: Settings, then Reset Account, wipes your progress on your phone straight away.
  • Cloud save: remove Ninja Rooftops data from your Google account in the Google Play Games app (Profile, then Settings, then Delete Play Games account and data, or remove a single game).
  • Ads: reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings under Google, then Ads.

More detail is in our Privacy Policy.
